# minimal_backdrop (Simplified Graphic 2D Backdrop Look)

**STYLE** (placed in `STYLE.txt` after `look: minimal_backdrop`):

look: minimal_backdrop
Hand-drawn digital 2D cel-shaded cartoon animation with clean Flash/ToonBoom keyframe aesthetics. Bold, clean, uniform black ink outlines with crisp vector-like edges. Flat, vibrant solid colors with minimal hard-edge cel-shading, zero 3D lighting, zero textures, zero gradients. Clean, minimalist flat two-tone 2D graphic backdrop with zero background clutter, placing 100% visual emphasis on the foreground character's pose-to-pose acting, hand gestures, and exaggerated facial expressions.
